Privacy Policy — Quick Clean

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Introduction

Quick Clean is a Chrome extension that lets you delete your browsing data (history, cookies, cache, etc.) directly from your browser. Your privacy is our top priority.

Data collected

Quick Clean does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

The extension works entirely locally in your browser. No information is sent to any external server.

Permissions used

The extension uses the following Chrome permissions, exclusively for its functionality:

  • browsingData — Delete browsing history, cache, form data and saved passwords.
  • cookies and host_permissions (all_urls) — Read and delete cookies from all sites, while respecting the user-configured whitelist.
  • history — Access browsing history in order to delete it.
  • tabs API — Used without the tabs permission to close open tabs when the option is enabled (no access to URLs or titles).
  • storage — Save your preferences locally (cleanup settings, whitelist).
  • notifications — Display a confirmation notification after each cleanup.

Local storage

The only data stored is your configuration preferences (which data to clean, time range, protected domains). This data stays in Chrome's local storage and never leaves your device.

Data sharing

Quick Clean does not share any data with third parties. The extension contains no analytics, tracking or advertising tools.
